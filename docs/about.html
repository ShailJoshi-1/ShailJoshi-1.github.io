03 / About

Curious across disciplines. Precise at the interfaces.

I’m Shail, a mechatronics engineering student at Toronto Metropolitan University. I like projects where CAD, circuits, controls, and code have to meet—and where the final answer has to survive outside a simulation.

S

I’m most engaged when a problem crosses boundaries: a camera result becomes a PLC decision, a digital pose becomes mechanical motion, or an electromagnetic model becomes a power-electronics requirement.

My approach is practical and iterative. I build enough of the full loop to expose the real problem, measure what happens, and improve the design with evidence.
