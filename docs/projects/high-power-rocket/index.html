Project anatomy

From problem definition through integration and validation.

01 / Context

Overview

Designed and CAD-modeled a rocket, using OpenRocket aerodynamic simulations to refine stability and predicted altitude across multiple design iterations. The vehicle integrates onboard circuitry, GPS tracking, and timed parachute deployment for flight monitoring and recovery.

02 / Goal

Objective

Design, launch, and recover a stable motor-propelled rocket, using simulation to guide the design and flight results to validate its predicted performance.

03 / Constraint

The challenge

Balance aerodynamic stability and altitude while integrating the motor, onboard electronics, GPS tracking, and parachute recovery into the airframe.

04 / Architecture

The solution

Iterated the rocket’s CAD design and evaluated flight stability and altitude in OpenRocket. Integrated onboard circuitry for timed parachute deployment and GPS tracking to support flight monitoring and safe recovery.

05 / Result

Outcome

The rocket reached 496 m (1,627 ft) during launch, validating the design calculations and enabling safe recovery with its onboard recovery and tracking systems.
